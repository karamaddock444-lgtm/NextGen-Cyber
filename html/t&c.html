Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Our NextGen Cyber Skills Hub values the privacy of our users and we are committed to protecting your personal information in accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). The POPIA principles that apply to our website are:

  • Accountability: Responsibility for using users' data lawfully.
  • Processing Limitation: Data is handled legally, fairly and only when necessary.
  • Purpose Specification: Data is collected for clear, specific purposes and is not kept longer than needed.
  • Further Processing Limitation: Users' data cannot be reused for new purposes unless it is compatible with the original purpose or consent has been obtained where required.
  • Information Quality: Users' data must be accurate, complete and up to date.
  • Openness: Users are informed about data collection, why it is being collected and how it will be processed.
  • Security Safeguards: Users' data is protected against loss, damage or unauthorised access.
  • Data Subject Participation: Users have the right to request access to and correction or deletion of their personal data, subject to applicable law.

NextGen Cyber Skills Hub makes minimal use of personal information, including full name, email address and message content submitted through our contact form. This information is used to respond to enquiries.
