Frequently Asked Questions


Find quick answers to common questions about NextGen Cyber Skills Hub, our workshops and learning opportunities.


If you cannot find the answer you are looking for, our team is always happy to help.

What is NextGen Cyber Skills Hub?

NextGen Cyber Skills Hub helps learners develop practical cybersecurity knowledge and future-ready digital skills.

Who can attend the workshops?

Our workshops are designed for learners interested in developing their cybersecurity knowledge and practical skills.

Do I need cybersecurity experience?

No. Our workshops cover different topics and skill levels, allowing learners to find sessions suited to their needs.

Are workshops available online?

Yes. NextGen Cyber offers online workshops as well as in-person sessions.

How do I book a workshop?

Select the Book Now button on the Workshops page. You can also contact us if you need more information.

Can I contact a mentor?

If you have a question about a workshop or learning opportunity, you can send us a message through the Contact page.

Still Have a Question?

Couldn't find the answer you were looking for? Get in touch with the NextGen Cyber team and send us your question or message.
